# Lato (LATO Labs)

> Agent-native research and simulation platform for investors. Lato's agents source and
> conduct hundreds of voice interviews with verified domain experts and customers in any
> language, combine them with thousands of public and proprietary documents into a single
> commercial study, and turn that study into a live market simulation analysts can question
> and run scenarios against. Used for commercial due diligence, expert interviews, market
> sizing, competitive mapping, pricing studies, deal sourcing, inbound screening and
> portfolio value creation. Founded by Tymek Staniszewski (CEO) and Tien Chu (CTO);
> backed by Y Combinator (Summer 2026); San Francisco.

## Status

Lato publishes no public developer API, API documentation, SDKs, or OpenAPI definition as
of 2026-07-19. `api.latolabs.io` and `docs.latolabs.io` do not serve a developer surface,
and `/docs`, `/api`, `/pricing`, `/blog`, `/llms.txt`, `/robots.txt` and `/sitemap.xml` all
return 404. This file is generated by the API Evangelist enrichment pipeline from the
company profile and repository artifacts, not published by Lato.

## Product integrations

Lato ingests from and connects to tools investment teams already use: Outlook, SharePoint,
Gmail, Google Drive, Slack, Teams, Excel, Affinity, Attio, Harmonic, and Granola. These are
inbound product integrations, not a Lato-provided API.
